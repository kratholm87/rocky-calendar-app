Privacy Policy — Kratholm Rocky

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Kratholm Rocky is a private application used only by its owner. No other users exist.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application accesses the owner's Google Calendar to read, create, update and delete calendar events.

Use and storage

Calendar data is used only to provide personal planning and reminders to the owner. Access credentials are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
